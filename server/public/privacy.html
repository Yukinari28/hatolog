Privacy Policy — HatoLog

Last updated: September 2026

HatoLog is a free, non-monetized fan-made companion app for the game Heartopia. This page explains what data the app collects and how it's used.

Data stored on your device

By default, everything you enter into HatoLog — your task tracker, cooking mastery progress, timers, and settings — is stored only in your own browser's local storage, on your own device. This data is never sent to us or anyone else unless you choose to sign in (see below).

Optional Google sign-in

If you choose to sign in with your Google account, HatoLog uses Firebase Authentication (a Google service) to verify your identity, and Firebase Firestore (a Google database service) to store a copy of your tracker/mastery data under your account, so it can sync across your devices.

When you sign in, we receive and store:

  • Your Google account's unique ID (used only to keep your data separate from other users')
  • Your name, email address, and profile picture, as provided by Google — shown only to you, inside the app, so you know which account you're signed into
  • Your HatoLog tracker/mastery data, exactly as it appears on your device

Sign-in is entirely optional. If you don't sign in, none of this data leaves your device.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell, share, or rent your data to anyone.
  • We don't run ads or any third-party trackers/analytics.
  • We don't use your data for anything other than making your own saved progress available to you across your own devices.

Deleting your data

You can clear your local data at any time from the app's Settings/Backup tab. If you've signed in and want your cloud-stored data deleted entirely, contact us using the link below and we'll remove it.

Third-party services

This app relies on Google Firebase for optional sign-in and sync. Your use of Google sign-in is also subject to Google's own Privacy Policy.

Contact

Questions or data deletion requests can be sent via the feedback link in the app (Discord: discord.gg/k2qtcKErWc).

HatoLog is an independent fan project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the makers of Heartopia.
